Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the internship take?

The internship is designed to be completed in 4 weeks with 10-15 hours of work per week. You have flexibility to work at your own pace.

What if I need more time?

We understand everyone learns differently. If you need more time, you can extend your access for an additional fee.

Is the certificate valid?

Yes, our certificates are verified and include a unique ID. While they're not government diplomas, they're recognized by many employers as proof of practical experience.

Can I choose my project?

Projects are assigned based on your chosen domain and current skill level to ensure the best learning experience.

What support is available?

You'll have access to mentors, community forums, help desk, and comprehensive learning resources throughout your internship.

Is there a refund policy?

Yes, we offer a 7-day money-back guarantee if you're not satisfied with the program quality.
